# Stakeholder, Decision Rights, and Agency

Roles:
- `DECIDER`
- `ADVISOR`
- `AUDITOR`
- `DELEGATE`
- `STAKEHOLDER`

Inspect:
- authority
- consent
- veto
- responsibility
- incentive conflict
- dependency
- coercion / duress
- accessibility
- capacity / agency

Do not infer the governing decision rule without evidence.
