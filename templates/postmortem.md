## Hindsight Protection Check

- Was the original decision-time record preserved without later information?
- Were decision-process quality and outcome quality assessed separately?
- Are missing original forecasts or outcomes marked `NOT ASSESSABLE` rather than reconstructed?
- Are conclusions limited to evidence actually supplied?
